# AI for Data Stewards

Proposed four-day course plan

## Purpose

A practical capability programme that teaches data stewards and adjacent professionals to use DIEZ’s seven AI products responsibly. Participants learn how each product works in the business, how to interpret its outputs, when human judgement is required and what evidence and controls must be retained.

Prepared for: Dubai Integrated Economic Zones Authority (DIEZ)

Format: Four consecutive full days, with pre-course diagnostic and post-course assessment

Planning basis: DIEZ operating context, UAE federal requirements and Dubai government data, digital and information-security instruments

Version: Proposed plan | September 2026

## DIEZ | AI for Data Stewards

## 1. Executive summary

This plan proposes a four-day applied course for DIEZ data stewards, business data owners, operational managers and business use-case owners. Its central purpose is to help participants use DIEZ’s seven AI products responsibly: Customer Segmentation, Sentiment Analysis, Customer Churn Prediction, Content Moderation, Partner IQ, Microsoft 365 Copilot and Document Intelligence. Participants are not expected to become AI engineers or data scientists.

The course connects each product to its real operating context, data inputs, output limitations, human decisions, controls and evidence. Participants practise how to interpret a customer tier, sentiment label, churn alert, moderation decision, partner recommendation, Copilot response or extracted document field without treating the result as unquestionable fact. DIEZ’s public positioning emphasises business enablement, digital transformation and advanced technologies, including AI-enabled operational improvement [3].

## Design principle

AI output informs work; it does not remove accountability. For every DIEZ product, participants learn to understand the output, check its fitness for the decision, apply the required human review, document the evidence and act or escalate. UAE and Dubai legal, policy and information-security controls are embedded in the product labs.

## 2. UAE and Dubai alignment

This course is an operational training design, not legal advice. Before delivery, DIEZ’s legal, data-protection, information-security and data-governance owners should confirm the exact instruments and internal policies that apply to each scenario, dataset and technology environment.

| Local requirement / context | How the course operationalises it |
| --- | --- |
| UAE Personal Data Protection Law — Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 [1] | Teach purpose limitation, data minimisation, access control, retention, processor/vendor review, incident escalation and cross-border transfer checks where personal data is in scope. The law expressly excludes government data and governmental entities that control or process personal data; the course therefore treats it as a baseline for provider/contractor and other in-scope processing, while pairing it with Dubai government instruments. |
| Dubai Law No. 26 of 2015 Regulating Data Dissemination and Exchange [2] | Use data inventory, classification, provenance, release/approval, authorised sharing and evidence of permitted use as mandatory steps in every data-readiness and capstone exercise. |
| Dubai Data and Statistics Establishment / Dubai Data framework [2] | Practise documenting data owners, custodians, quality issues, metadata, reuse conditions and escalation routes for datasets used in government or cross-entity workflows. |
| Dubai Law No. 9 of 2022 Regulating the Provision of Digital Services [2] | Frame AI use around service quality, user impact, accessibility, continuity, human escalation and accountable change control for digital government services. |
| Dubai ICT Policies — Executive Council Resolution No. 15 of 2022 [2] | Require participants to identify the approved platform, identity/access path, hosting and integration constraints, records/evidence needs and responsible owner before proposing an AI-enabled workflow. |
| Dubai Information Security Regulation — Executive Council Resolution No. 13 of 2012 [2] | Embed least privilege, secure handling, no unapproved public upload, logging/evidence, incident reporting and controlled testing into labs and capstones. |
| Dubai's digital-government and economic-zone context [3] | Use the actual DIEZ product contexts: customer tiers in Salesforce, customer-care touchpoints, churn intervention, the Dubai CommerCity community app, free-zone partner matching, Microsoft 365 work and government-service document processing. |

## 4. Intended outcomes

By the end of the course, participants should be able to:

 Explain what each of the seven DIEZ AI products does, where its output appears and which business decision or workflow it supports.

 Interpret customer tiers, sentiment labels, confidence scores, churn alerts, moderation classifications, partner rankings, Copilot responses and extracted document fields without treating them as certainty.

 Identify the data definitions, quality conditions and ownership required for each product to operate responsibly.

 Use Microsoft 365 Copilot responsibly in PowerPoint, Excel, Word and Outlook with approved information and appropriate verification.

 Verify product outputs and decide whether to accept, investigate, override, correct or escalate them.

 Recognise privacy, confidentiality, hallucination, bias, security, intellectual-property and over-reliance risks.

 Apply product-specific human review, decision rights, evidence retention, exception handling and monitoring.

 Create a responsible-use operating procedure for one DIEZ product and a practical 30-day application plan.

## 5. DIEZ product operating picture and lab focus

The seven products are the organising spine of the programme. Known operational facts are taught directly. Where the technical mechanism or owner has not been confirmed, the course labels the point as unconfirmed and teaches participants to seek authoritative clarification rather than infer how the product works.

| Product | What it does and how it works | Owner / status | Responsible-use lab |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Customer Segmentation | Assigns diamond, platinum, gold or bronze tiers. An unsupervised model uses about 11-12 drivers; an integration script places the tier in Salesforce. | Owner not stated. | Reconcile competing customer definitions; create a governed tier definition and challenge inconsistent inputs. |
| 2. Sentiment Analysis | Classifies inquiries, complaints and tickets as positive, neutral or negative and returns a confidence score. | Customer Care. | Set confidence thresholds; test Arabic and English text; define what the score can and cannot justify. |
| 3. Customer Churn Prediction | Predicts customers likely to leave, including progressive lease reductions. Target is an alert at least three months ahead. | Customer Care. | Treat the alert as probability; define validation, leadership-visit and discount decision rules; record the human decision. |
| 4. Content Moderation | Screens text and images in the Dubai CommerCity community app. | Owner not stated. Azure Content Moderation is an unconfirmed assumption. | Translate policy into rules; test false positives and negatives; design review and escalation without assuming the technology. |
| 5. Partner IQ | Matches businesses within the same free zone so members can source services from neighbouring zone companies. | Owner and technical mechanism not confirmed; recommendation engine is only a working hypothesis. | Define matching criteria; challenge ranking bias and unequal visibility; require an explainable and contestable recommendation. |
| 6. Microsoft 365 Copilot | Supports work in PowerPoint, Excel, Word and Outlook through the licensed Microsoft 365 Copilot environment. | Digital. | Use approved data, verify sources and calculations, protect confidentiality and retain human accountability for the final work. |
| 7. Document Intelligence | Uses OCR to extract fields from documents such as visa applications and populate another government entity's portal; it is not a RAG workflow. | Operational ownership to confirm; Government Services is indicated by the use case. | Check extraction accuracy and missing fields; route exceptions; retain source-to-field evidence and protect personal data. |


> Product-fact discipline: unconfirmed owners and technologies must remain visibly marked “to confirm”; participants must not convert assumptions into operational facts.

## 7. Capstone design

Each participant or small group develops a responsible-use operating procedure for one of the seven DIEZ products. The procedure must be operationally credible but can be tested with approved, anonymised or simulated information rather than production data.

| Capstone component | Required question | Minimum evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product and purpose | Which DIEZ product is in scope and what approved business purpose does it serve? | Product, workflow, users and approved-purpose statement. |
| Output and decision | What output does the product produce, and what remains human-owned? | Output interpretation and human-accountability statement. |
| Data readiness | Is the data documented, governed, accessible and fit for purpose? | Readiness checklist and gaps. |
| Risk and controls | What could go wrong, and what controls reduce the risk? | Risk review, safe-use decisions and evidence rules. |
| Human oversight | Who reviews, decides, approves exceptions and escalates? | Decision-rights and escalation map. |
| Workflow | How does the work run end to end? | Input → assistance → review → decision → evidence → action → monitoring. |
| Implementation | What is needed to adopt this safely? | Dependencies, roles, change actions and 30-day experiment. |
| Measures | How will value, quality, safety and adoption be monitored? | Leading and lagging indicators. |


## Recommended capstone scenarios

Leasing operations; real-estate pricing support; government-service request handling; customer growth or retention; compliance triage; operational document processing. Final scenario pack to be confirmed with DIEZ.

## 8. Assessment and evidence model

Assessment is developmental and evidence-based. The pre-assessment is not a pass/fail gate; it calibrates the cohort and establishes a baseline.

| Stage | Method | Evidence / purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before | 30-45 minute diagnostic and confidence survey across the seven products. | Baseline of product familiarity, current use, limitations awareness, data-governance awareness and role responsibility. |
| During | Facilitator observation, peer review, pulse checks, daily artefact checks and scenario challenges. | Checks conceptual understanding and practical stewardship decisions as they develop. |
| Final | Product-use scenario assessment, capstone review and 30-day transfer plan. | Measures progress from baseline and confirms the participant can operate a DIEZ product within defined responsible-use controls. |
| Optional follow-up | Manager check-in or community-of-practice session after 30 days. | Captures implementation progress, blockers and evidence of adoption. |


Suggested capstone rubric (to confirm with DIEZ)

| Criterion | Weight | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem framing and value | 20% | Specific user or process problem; credible benefit; appropriate AI boundary. |
| Data readiness | 20% | Data documentation, access, quality and representation are assessed with gaps stated. |
| Responsible controls | 25% | Risks, privacy/security, human review, decision rights and escalation are explicit. |
| Workflow and implementation | 20% | The workflow is repeatable, feasible and supported by roles, dependencies and adoption actions. |
| Measures and evidence | 15% | Success measures cover value, quality, safety, adoption and audit evidence. |

## 9. Learning resources and participant toolkit

The following resources are recommended for each participant. They are designed to support both the course and workplace transfer.

 Participant workbook with plain-language AI concepts, guided exercises, reflection prompts and role-specific application space.

 AI practice toolkit: prompt canvas, output-verification checklist, safe-use decision guide, risk-review template and human-oversight template.

 Stewardship scenario pack: simulated datasets, metadata records, issue logs, governance requests and DIEZ- relevant cases.

 Transfer pack: product quick-reference sheets, responsible-use checklist, glossary, capstone template, evidence guide and 30-day application plan.

 Facilitator pack: session plans, worked examples, challenge cards, observation checklist, assessment materials and marking guidance.

 Final cohort size, participant roles and any requirements for separate manager, steward or business-owner cohorts.

 Whether delivery is four consecutive days or two two-day blocks within two weeks.

 Microsoft 365 Copilot licences, identity/access arrangements and the policy for using simulated, anonymised or authorised information.

## 13. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation in this plan |
| --- | --- |
| Participants use sensitive data in an unapproved tool. | Use safe, authorised or simulated information; define tool and data rules during mobilisation; include a safe-use decision guide. |
| Course becomes tool training rather than capability building. | Use a tool-neutral discipline and scenario evidence; treat tools as examples inside a broader workflow. |
| Outputs are accepted without verification. | Make review and evidence capture mandatory in every lab; assess accept/revise/reject/escalate decisions. |

## 14. Recommended decision

Proceed with the four-day product-responsibility course as the baseline for client discussion, subject to a mobilisation workshop with DIEZ. The workshop should validate the seven product fact sheets, confirm owners and currently unconfirmed technologies, agree safe demonstration access, define decision thresholds and escalation routes, and approve the bilingual and measurement approach. Once confirmed, the timetable can be converted into a facilitator guide, participant workbook, product lab pack and assessment pack without changing the core learning journey.
