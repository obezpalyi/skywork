## 1. About these briefs

These briefs describe each session of the four-day programme: what participants do, which DIEZ initiative it draws on, what they produce and which outcome it evidences. They follow the day themes, working disciplines and evidence artefacts of the submitted Programme Summary and do not change them.

How to read them:

• Session IDs run D1.S1 to D4.S6. Durations are net contact time; clock times and breaks are set with DIEZ HR.

• Participants work in teams formed by function across the three zones, so that the same process can be compared across DAFZ, DSO and Dubai CommerCity.

• “The approved assistant” is DIEZ's Microsoft 365 Copilot, used with simulated data only. It is DIEZ's approved environment, not the subject of the training.

• No input block runs longer than 20 minutes; every session ends with an artefact for the participant's evidence portfolio.

• Output names match the participant toolkit in the Programme Summary.

• Key terms in each brief feed the bilingual glossary for Arabic co-facilitation.

• References such as G1–G8 point to the assumptions on DIEZ's data-governance and AI-governance frameworks in Appendix D. Those frameworks have not yet been shared.

## 2. Outcomes referenced in the briefs

DIEZ Digital described six outcomes for data stewards on 25 September 2026. They map onto the learning outcomes of the Programme Summary and are the basis for the baseline and post-assessment items.

| ID | After the programme, participants can... |
| --- | --- |
| O1 | Know which AI governance practices come with a product they are asked to sign off. |
| O2 | Tell a responsible AI product or output from one that is not: privacy, security, bias, human oversight, verification. |
| O3 | Triage a use case: safe to adopt; adopt only with a human in the loop; not solvable by AI yet. |
| O4 | Explain how the quality of the data they own drives the quality of AI built on it. |
| O5 | Understand DIEZ's seven live AI initiatives and frame new use cases from their own pain points. |
| O6 | Know the danger of putting sensitive data into non-approved AI tools, and act accordingly. |

## 4. Day 1 — Understand and Identify

Day purpose: Build a shared, plain-language model of AI through DIEZ's own initiatives, set the rules for safe use, and select worthwhile opportunities.

Day evidence: DIEZ AI initiative map, AI Opportunity Map, tested working pattern.

**D1.S1 · Welcome, baseline read-out and learning contract**

| Duration · lead · format | 30 min · Softengi, with Wellington · Plenary |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Connect the programme to what participants said in the baseline diagnostic and agree the rules for working with AI during the four days. |
| DIEZ anchor | Baseline diagnostic; the three free zones and the functions represented in the room. |
| What participants do | 1. Hear the cohort's baseline themes: confidence, current AI use, main concerns.2. Form function teams across zones, for example licensing heads from DAFZ, DSO and Dubai CommerCity together.3. Agree the working rules: approved assistant only, simulated data only, verify before use.4. Write personal success criteria for the four days. |
| Materials | Baseline summary (Wellington); team list. |
| Output | Personal success criteria; learning contract; evidence portfolio opened. |
| Outcomes | Baseline for O1-O6 |
| Responsible-AI checkpoint | Working rules for the whole programme. |
| Key terms (glossary) | baseline, evidence portfolio, data owner, data steward |
| Needs from DIEZ | Participant list with zone, function and product owned; baseline results. |


**D1.S2 · AI through DIEZ's own seven initiatives**

| Duration - lead - format | 60 min · Softengi · 20 min input, 40 min team mapping |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Build a plain-language understanding of what AI does by looking at the seven initiatives DIEZ already runs, not at vendors or products. |
| DIEZ anchor | All seven initiatives, grouped by capability: grouping (Customer Segmentation), classifying (Sentiment Analysis, Content Moderation), predicting (Customer Churn Prediction), matching (Partner IQ), extracting (Document Intelligence), generating (Copilot). |
| What participants do | 1. Short input: what each capability does, where it is strong, where it fails; predictive versus generative AI.2. Each team takes the initiatives closest to its function and completes one map row: business purpose, data used, business owner, what a wrong output would look like, who would notice.3. Gallery walk: teams add to each other's rows. |
| Materials | One card per initiative, based on DIEZ's own descriptions; screenshots where provided. |
| Output | DIEZ AI initiative map. |
| Outcomes | O5, O2 |
| Responsible-AI checkpoint | Every row names who is accountable for the output. |
| Key terms (glossary) | model, prediction, probability, classification, confidence, hallucination |
| Needs from DIEZ | Business owner of each initiative; screenshots or user-guide pages. |

**D1.S5 · Lab: context and instruction on a stewardship task**

| Duration · lead · format | 75 min · Softengi · 10 min briefing, 55 min lab, 10 min debrief |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Practise giving an assistant enough context to produce useful stewardship work, and see how weak instructions produce weak outputs. |

| Needs from DIEZ | DIEZ data-dictionary or metadata template, if one exists. |

**D2.S4 · Lab B: Customer Care — Sentiment Analysis and Churn Prediction**

| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Work with two model outputs that Customer Care relies on and decide where human review is needed. |

| Outcomes | O2, O3, O4 |

## D.1 Data governance and AI governance

DIEZ's data-governance framework and AI-governance framework have not yet been shared with the faculty. DIEZ Digital confirmed on 25 September 2026 that DIEZ's data-governance team owns these frameworks and that the training must stay within them.

• Each assumption is replaced by DIEZ's own wording and structure when the documents arrive.

• No external framework, standard or legal instrument is presented as DIEZ policy.

• Where DIEZ has no written policy on a point, the session treats it as an open question for the data owner and Digital to settle. Participants' artefacts (checklists, escalation maps, sign-off criteria) can be offered to DIEZ's governance team as input. The faculty does not write DIEZ policy.
