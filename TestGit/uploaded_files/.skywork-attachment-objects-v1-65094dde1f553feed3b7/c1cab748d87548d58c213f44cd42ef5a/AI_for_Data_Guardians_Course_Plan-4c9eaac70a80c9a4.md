# AI for Data Stewards: Strategic Enablement & Governance

## A Comprehensive 14-Session Curriculum for Data Specialists

> Source: shared document `AI_for_Data_Guardians_Course_Plan.html`

## 1. Program Overview & Objectives

This curriculum is designed for data specialists who already possess foundational AI knowledge. Moving beyond basic definitions, the course focuses on the intersection of data stewardship, AI governance, and strategic business value.

The program consists of **14 sessions (60–90 minutes each)**, structured with two intensive onsite workshops bookending a series of specialized online modules.

### Target skills addressed

- **AI literacy & safe usage:** Elevate awareness of AI capabilities, safe usage protocols, and proactive opportunity identification.
- **Enable high-quality AI use cases:** Identify, frame, and sponsor AI use cases that are technically feasible, data-ready, and aligned with organizational priorities.
- **Reduce AI delivery and governance risk:** Build awareness of data-readiness prerequisites, algorithmic limitations, ethical considerations, and regulatory compliance.
- **Drive adoption and time-to-value:** Help users understand, trust, and act on AI outputs by embedding AI into decision-making.

## 2. Detailed Course Plan

### Module 1: Foundations & Opportunity Identification (Onsite Workshop 1)

This initial onsite workshop sets the strategic baseline, fosters collaboration, and aligns data stewards with the organization’s AI vision.

#### Session 1: The Evolving Role of the Data Steward in the AI Era

- **Delivery:** Onsite
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Skill:** AI literacy & safe usage
- **Content:** Transitioning from traditional data governance to AI data stewardship; overview of AI strategy and acceptable-use policies.
- **Structure:** 30-minute lecture, 40-minute interactive group discussion on governance bottlenecks, and 20-minute Q&A.

#### Session 2: Identifying and Sponsoring High-Value AI Use Cases

- **Delivery:** Onsite
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Skill:** Enable high-quality AI use cases
- **Content:** Frameworks for spotting AI opportunities and differentiating feasible, high-ROI projects from hype.
- **Structure:** 45-minute methodology presentation and 45-minute breakout workshop to brainstorm and pitch use-case ideas.

### Module 2: Data Readiness & Problem Framing (Online)

This module focuses on the technical and structural prerequisites for successful AI deployment.

#### Session 3: Assessing Data Readiness for AI Models

- **Delivery:** Online
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Skill:** Reduce delivery risk
- **Content:** Define “data-ready”; evaluate data quality, volume, velocity, and lineage requirements for machine learning and generative AI.
- **Structure:** Case study of an AI project that failed because of poor data readiness, followed by a checklist walkthrough.

#### Session 4: Framing the AI Problem & Defining Success Metrics

- **Delivery:** Online
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Skill:** Enable high-quality AI use cases
- **Content:** Translate business problems into AI/ML problems; establish baselines and define “good” using accuracy, precision, recall, and business ROI.
- **Structure:** Interactive webinar with live polling and a framing exercise using a dataset scenario.

#### Session 5: Feasibility Analysis & Resource Scoping

- **Delivery:** Online
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Skill:** Enable high-quality AI use cases
- **Content:** Assess technical and operational feasibility; understand the compute, talent, and time needed to move from proof of concept to production.
- **Structure:** Expert presentation followed by Q&A with a senior AI product manager.

### Module 3: Governance, Risk, and Ethics (Online)

These sessions safeguard the organization against risks introduced by artificial intelligence.

#### Session 6: Understanding AI Limitations & Hallucinations

- **Delivery:** Online
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Skill:** Reduce governance risk
- **Content:** Model degradation, drift, and large-language-model hallucinations; how data stewards can monitor and flag these issues.
- **Structure:** Technical walkthrough of model-monitoring dashboards and real-world AI failure examples.

#### Session 7: AI Ethics, Bias Mitigation, and Fairness

- **Delivery:** Online
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Skill:** Reduce governance risk
- **Content:** Identify historical bias in training data and apply techniques for measuring and mitigating bias.
- **Structure:** 45-minute lecture and 45-minute interactive case study on algorithmic bias in financial or HR decision-making.

#### Session 8: Regulatory Requirements & Compliance Landscape

- **Delivery:** Online
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Skill:** Reduce governance risk
- **Content:** Overview of global AI regulations, including the EU AI Act and local privacy laws; the data steward’s role in compliance and auditability.
- **Structure:** Guest lecture from Legal/Compliance followed by Q&A.

#### Session 9: Safe Usage, Security Protocols, and IP Protection

- **Delivery:** Online
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Skill:** AI literacy & safe usage
- **Content:** Secure AI data pipelines, prevent leakage through third-party APIs, and protect intellectual property.
- **Structure:** Threat-modeling exercise and review of internal security protocols.

### Module 4: Driving Adoption & Time-to-Value (Online)

This module focuses on ensuring that AI tools are trusted, adopted, and effectively used by the business.

#### Session 10: Explainability: Understanding and Trusting AI Outputs

- **Delivery:** Online
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Skill:** Drive adoption & time-to-value
- **Content:** Introduction to explainable AI; interpret model outputs and communicate the “why” behind AI decisions to non-technical stakeholders.
- **Structure:** Demonstration of explainability concepts and tools such as SHAP and LIME.

#### Session 11: Embedding AI into Decision-Making Workflows

- **Delivery:** Online
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Skill:** Drive adoption & time-to-value
- **Content:** Move from standalone dashboards to integrated workflows; design human-in-the-loop systems for critical decisions.
- **Structure:** Workflow-mapping exercise using digital whiteboards.

#### Session 12: Change Management for AI Adoption

- **Delivery:** Online
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Skill:** Drive adoption & time-to-value
- **Content:** Overcome organizational resistance, upskill end users, and foster a data-driven culture.
- **Structure:** Panel discussion with business leaders who have driven AI adoption.

### Module 5: Capstone & Assessment (Onsite Workshop 2)

The final onsite workshop assesses learning outcomes and provides a forum for program evaluation.

#### Session 13: Capstone Presentations: Pitching a Governed AI Use Case

- **Delivery:** Onsite
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Skill:** Comprehensive assessment
- **Content:** Present a fully framed AI use case developed throughout the course, addressing data readiness, risk mitigation, and adoption strategy.
- **Structure:** Ten-minute presentations per group followed by feedback from senior leaders acting as sponsors.

#### Session 14: Program Review, Feedback, and Continuous Improvement

- **Delivery:** Onsite
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Skill:** Program effectiveness
- **Content:** Review key takeaways, collect structured feedback, discuss next steps, and identify continuous learning paths.
- **Structure:** Facilitated retrospective, detailed feedback survey, and closing remarks.

## 3. Assessment, Feedback & Reporting Strategy

- **Participant progress:** Use the Session 13 capstone as the primary practical assessment. Add three-question pulse checks at the end of each online session.
- **Formative feedback:** Collect anonymous digital polls after Modules 2 and 3 to support mid-course corrections.
- **Summative feedback:** Use a standardized Session 14 survey covering NPS, content relevance, and instructor effectiveness.
- **Final summary report:** Within two weeks of completion, report cohort performance and engagement, the portfolio of generated AI use cases, aggregated feedback, and recommendations for curriculum improvement.

## 4. Recommendations Based on Successful AI Literacy Projects

| Recommendation | Rationale and implementation |
|---|---|
| Establish an “AI Champions” network | Identify high-performing data stewards to serve as peer resources in their departments, reducing the bottleneck on central data-science teams. |
| Implement a “Use Case Sandbox” | Provide a secure, ring-fenced environment where participants can explore LLMs or ML models with synthetic company data. |
| Gamify risk identification | Use a red-teaming exercise in Sessions 6–9 to find flaws, bias, and security loopholes in a dummy AI model. |
| Create continuous-learning micro-modules | Offer a quarterly 30-minute AI Update webinar covering regulation, model capabilities, and changes to data-readiness standards. |
